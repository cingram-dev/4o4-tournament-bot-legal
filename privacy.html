Privacy Policy

4o4 Esports Tournament Bot • Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the 4o4 Esports Tournament Bot ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how you can request deletion of your data.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot only collects the minimum data needed to run Discord tournaments synced with Start.gg:

  • Discord Identifiers: Discord User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, Thread IDs, and Message IDs needed to run match check-ins, announcements, and dispute threads.
  • Player Profile Data (User-Provided or Synced): Start.gg gamerTags, Start.gg entrant/user IDs, and voluntary in-game identifiers (such as CFN / Capcom ID) submitted via /settag or /link.
  • Tournament & Match Records: Check-in timestamps, reported match scores, disqualification status, and dispute descriptions submitted through Bot commands or modals.

We do not collect private direct messages outside Bot interactions, passwords, payment details, or unrelated server chat history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Match your Discord account to your Start.gg bracket sets so opponents can find and invite you.
  • Manage round starts, check-in timers, automated DQs, and two-way score verification.
  • Sync verified match results back to Start.gg.
  • Notify Tournament Organizers when a player submits a match dispute or help request.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Match results, check-in states, and dispute rulings are shared only with:

  • Discord (to display match cards, threads, and announcements in participating tournament channels).
  • Start.gg (to fetch bracket pairings and submit verified match outcomes for events you entered).
  • Tournament Organizers of the Discord server hosting the event.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored in a local SQLite database maintained by the Bot operator. Linked profile tags are retained to make future tournament check-ins faster; tournament match and dispute logs are retained for bracket verification and historical tournament records, or until deleted upon request.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may update your stored in-game tag or Start.gg gamerTag at any time using /settag or /link. To request full deletion of your stored profile or tournament records from the Bot database, please contact a 4o4 Esports Tournament Organizer in Discord or submit a request via the Bot's GitHub repository. Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended only for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements (13+ or higher depending on local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below Discord's minimum age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
